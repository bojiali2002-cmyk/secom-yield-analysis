# Data sources and attribution

SECOM: Michael McCann and Adrian Johnston (2008), UCI Machine Learning Repository. DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C54305 . Dataset page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/179/secom . Raw data are omitted from this repository; obtain them from UCI and retain its attribution.
